    What is WordOnTheStreet?

    WordOnTheStreet is a real-time sentiment analyzer that fetches recent Reddit comments on any keyword and calculates an average sentiment score using VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner).

    The aggregate compound score ranges from -1.0 (Very Negative) to +1.0 (Very Positive), providing a quick snapshot of current public perception.

    Limitations & Disclaimer

    Please treat scores as an estimate rather than absolute fact. Because VADER relies on a lexicon and rule-based model rather than full context comprehension:

    • Targeting Ambiguity: It evaluates the overall text rather than who the sentiment is aimed at.
      "The ban on {KEYWORD} is terrible" will register as negative, even if the user is defending the keyword.
    • Sarcasm & Slang: Sarcasm, ironical memes, and newer internet slang often bypass standard dictionary rules.
    • Sample Bias: Results reflect recent Reddit activity and may not capture broader offline sentiment.
